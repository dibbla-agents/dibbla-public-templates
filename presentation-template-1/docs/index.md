# Your slide deck

This app is a presentation deck. Open it to view your slides full screen, move
between them with the keyboard, and export the whole deck to PDF.

> This handbook is an **example** shipped with the template. Rewrite it for your
> own deck before you deploy — it is the only documentation your end users see.

## How to use it

- **Move between slides** with the arrow keys.
- **Go full screen** for presenting.
- **Export to PDF** to share the deck as a file.

## Getting help

If a slide looks wrong, reopen the app from your **My Apps** page. Edit your
slides, redeploy, and hard-reload the browser to see the changes.
