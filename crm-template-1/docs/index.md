# Welcome to your CRM

This app helps you keep track of your contacts, the tasks you owe them, and the
campaigns you are running — all from one dashboard.

> This handbook is an **example** shipped with the template. Rewrite it for your
> own app before you deploy — it is the only documentation your end users see.

## What you can do

- **Dashboard.** See your key numbers at a glance.
- **Contacts.** Keep a list of the people you work with.
- **Tasks.** Track what needs doing and when.
- **Campaigns.** Follow the campaigns you are running.

## Getting help

If a page looks empty, that is normal before you have added data. Reopen the app
from your **My Apps** page any time, and redeploy to publish changes.
