# Welcome

This is a minimal starter web app. It shows a simple greeting today, and it is
ready for you to build on.

> This handbook is an **example** shipped with the template. Rewrite it for your
> own app before you deploy — it is the only documentation your end users see.

## What this app does

Right now it displays a friendly hello message. That is the blank canvas: tell
the assistant what you want the app to do, and it grows from here.

## Getting help

If the page does not load, reopen the app from your **My Apps** page. When you
make changes, redeploy and hard-reload your browser to see the latest version.
